>
> ## Problem 3: Insurance Verifcation
>
> Develop a solution to simplify and automate insurance verification for dental practices.

### Why this matters

Dental clinics spend significant administrative time verifying insurance eligibility, benefits, and procedure coverage. Manual verification can delay appointments, increase repetitive workload for front-desk staff, and make it harder to provide patients with clear cost expectations. DENSURE addresses this by bringing insurance verification, CDT procedure selection, coverage analysis, and patient cost estimation into a single workflow—helping dental teams work more efficiently while giving patients clearer information before treatment.
---

## Our Solution
**DENSURE** is an intelligent dental insurance verification platform that simplifies the entire process through one unified dashboard. It allows staff select CDT procedures, verify eligibility, analyze coverage, and estimate patient costs. Unlike traditional manual methods, DENSURE combines automation, AI-powered explanations, smart alerts, and report generation to reduce repetitive work, improve efficiency, and provide clearer insurance information to dental clinics and patients.

## Key Features

- **Feature 1** — Automated Insurance Eligibility Verification
Quickly checks whether a patient's dental insurance policy is Active, Expired, or Cancelled.
Replaces the manual verification process with an automated workflow.
- **Feature 2** — Search using a patient's Member ID.
Displays patient name, DOB, insurance provider, and policy status.
- **Feature 3** — Procedure-Specific Coverage
Shows coverage for individual dental procedures such as:
Cleaning,
Fillings,
Crowns,
Root canals,
Extractions
- **Feature 4** —  Deductible & Annual Maximum Calculation
Takes the remaining deductible into account.
Tracks the remaining annual insurance limit.
